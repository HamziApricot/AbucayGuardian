A SOS emergency application for the municipality of Abucay, Bataan. It is called "Abucay Guardian".

## Features

1. SOS emergency button (when pressed, it will notify all the family members, and 911 hotline)
2. Hotlines 
3. Procedures
4. GPS family tracking
5. Family members
6. Emergency Plan

## Pages

1. Home (Quick actions, emergency plan, hotlines, procedures, big sos button
2. SOS
3. Hotlines
4. Procedures
5. Family Tracker
6. Family members
7. Emergency Plan

## Navigations

1. Home
2. Hotlines
3. Procedures
4. Tracker
5. Family
6. Profile

## Design 

1. Primary Color: White
2. Secondary Color: Dark Blue 
3. SOS button: Red
4. Tracker: Green
5. Hotline: Orange 
6. Family: Blue
7. Emergency Plan: Yellow
